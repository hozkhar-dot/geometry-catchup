# Geometry Catch-Up Plan

**Purpose:** Close a missing geometry year (skipped due to a school year abroad) for a strong precalculus student, targeted at the SAT/ACT and at supporting precalc trigonometry.
**Timeline:** September 8 to mid-December 2026, about 14 weeks.
**Cadence:** Three sessions per week, 45 minutes each. Two sessions of new material, one of practice problems on that week's topics.
**Primary resource:** Khan Academy, free (mainly the "High school geometry" course, plus "Basic geometry and measurement" for foundations). Unit names below match Khan's current organization; if a name differs slightly, search the topic directly.

> Guiding rule: this is test-and-classroom geometry, not a full school course. Skip formal proofs, constructions, and transformation proofs entirely. Conic sections other than circles are precalc's job, not this plan's.

---

## The checklist, in order

### Block 1: Foundations (Weeks 1-2)

- [ ] Angle basics: complementary, supplementary, vertical angles
- [ ] Parallel lines cut by a transversal (alternate interior, corresponding angles)
- [ ] Angle sums: triangle (180°), polygon interior/exterior angles
- Khan: Basic geometry and measurement → angle units; High school geometry → congruence intro topics

### Block 2: Triangles I (Weeks 3-4)

- [ ] Pythagorean theorem, including word problems
- [ ] Special right triangles: 45-45-90 and 30-60-90
- [ ] Triangle inequality and side-angle relationships
- Khan: High school geometry → Right triangles & trigonometry (first sections); Pythagorean theorem units

### Block 3: Triangles II, similarity (Weeks 5-6)

- [ ] Congruent triangles (concept level, no proofs)
- [ ] Similar triangles: setting up and solving ratios
- [ ] Scale factors for lengths, areas, volumes
- Khan: High school geometry → Congruence; Similarity

### Block 4: Right-triangle trigonometry (Weeks 7-8)

- [ ] SOH-CAH-TOA: sine, cosine, tangent as ratios
- [ ] Solving right triangles (find a side, find an angle)
- [ ] Angles of elevation and depression word problems
- Khan: High school geometry → Right triangles & trigonometry
- **Note:** this block feeds directly into precalculus trig. If precalc reaches trig before Week 7, pull this block forward.

### Block 5: Circles (Weeks 9-10)

- [ ] Circumference and area
- [ ] Arc length and sector area
- [ ] Central and inscribed angles
- [ ] Tangent lines (radius ⊥ tangent)
- [ ] Equation of a circle in the coordinate plane
- Khan: High school geometry → Circles; Analytic geometry (circle equations)

### Block 6: Coordinate geometry (Week 11)

- [ ] Slope, parallel and perpendicular slopes
- [ ] Distance formula and midpoint formula
- [ ] Applying these to triangles and circles on the plane
- Khan: High school geometry → Analytic geometry

### Block 7: Solid geometry (Week 12)

- [ ] Volume: prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, spheres
- [ ] Surface area basics
- [ ] Density and unit-conversion word problems
- Khan: High school geometry → Solid geometry

### Block 8: Mixed review and testing (Weeks 13-14)

- [ ] Mixed problem sets across all blocks
- [ ] College Board Student Question Bank: "Geometry and Trigonometry" domain sets (official SAT questions)
- [ ] ACT practice geometry questions, timed
- [ ] Redo anything below 75 percent accuracy

## Checkpoints

| When | Check | Target |
|---|---|---|
| End of Block 2 | 10 Pythagorean/special-triangle problems, timed | 8 of 10 |
| End of Block 4 | 10 right-triangle trig problems | 7 of 10 |
| End of Block 5 | 10 circle problems | 7 of 10 |
| Week 13-14 | 20 mixed official questions (SAT Question Bank + ACT), timed | 75%+ |

If a checkpoint misses its target, repeat that block's practice session before moving on. Falling one week behind schedule is fine; skipping a shaky block is not.

## Calendar alignment

- **October 1, SAT (school day):** the catch-up will be only ~3 weeks in. Expectations for the math section should reflect that; the score is a baseline.
- **October, PSAT/NMSQT:** same. Diagnostic only.
- **December, national ACT (optional):** by then Blocks 1-7 are done. If the October results support the ACT path, this is the natural first official ACT. Verify the exact December test date and registration deadline at act.org (registration typically closes about five weeks before).
- **Ongoing:** precalculus trig gets easier from Block 4 onward.

## What this plan deliberately ignores

Formal two-column proofs, geometric constructions, transformation proofs, and conics beyond the circle. None of it appears meaningfully on the SAT or ACT, and precalc covers conics when needed. Every hour saved goes to triangles, trig, and circles, which is where the test points and the classroom payoff live.
